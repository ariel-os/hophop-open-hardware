A simple 3-layer plywood case for the Nymph board (when used with a 10x45mm antenna on a 55mm cable),
also compatible with the makerdiary nRF9151 Connect Kit (with the antennas shipped with it).

## Materials
* 4mm plywood (9x16cm, depending on arrangement)
* Laser cutter
* Wood glue
* a 7x7 square nut
* a M4x12 flat-head screw

## Manufacturing
* Cut the plywood in a laser cutter into the shapes laid out separately in `cut.svg`.
  Take care to not burn too much, especially when movement speed is limited due to sharp corners (otherwise the LED grids burn out completely).
* Glue the bottom two layers together.
  Take care to align the prongs of the middle layer well: they bend inwards easily, and then the PCB would not fit any more. Align them with the outer side of the base layer.
* Insert the board and antenna.
* Insert the nut in the square hole at the bottom, and screw closed from the top.

## CAD implementation

The inner shapes available in the SVG file were exported through OpenSCAD as described in the `Makefile`.
OpenSCAD is used here to slice the STL generated by KiCAD into projections that cover groups of 4mm.

## Modifications and future development

When used with the Nymph (which has not components at the bottom), the holes in the lowest plate can be left out.

Support for other nuts can be added as necessary.

Thicknesses are not quite ideal:
At the current 4mm, the USB and uFL connectors can just not be covered completely.
At 3mm, the power connector of the Nymph would protrude over the top side. A 4th layer might be added then that would cover the USB port.
Layers of 1.5mm or 2mm (eg. Kraftplex) would work with 5-6 (USB open / USB covered; battery connector still protruding) or 4-5 layers.
